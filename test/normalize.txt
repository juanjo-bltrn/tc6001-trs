The following propositions should be normalized.

?- normalise_cnf(p <-> q, F).
F = (¬p\/q)/\(¬q\/p).

?- normalise_cnf(¬ (p /\ (q \/ ¬ r)), F).
F = (¬p\/¬q)/\(¬p\/r).

?- normalise_cnf((a -> b) -> c, F).
F = (a\/c)/\(¬b\/c).

On the other hand, the following non-well formed propositons should not be normalized.

?- normalise_cnf(p + q, F).
false.

?- normalise_cnf(p /\ ¬ q \/ R, F).
false.

?- normalise_cnf(p /\ ¬ (q , r), F).
false.
